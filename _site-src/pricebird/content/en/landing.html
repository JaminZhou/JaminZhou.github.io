What PriceBird does

Compare by item, weight or volume

Enter each package the way it is sold and compare the unit price on one consistent basis.

Up to four products at once

Line up a shelf's worth of options side by side instead of comparing them two at a time.

Coupons, deposits, full-reduction

Real-world price adjustments are part of the calculation, not a footnote you apply in your head.

Local history

Past calculations and custom units stay on your device for the next trip to the shop.

Currency reference

Convert to your home currency for reference while comparing imported or travel purchases.

Siri and Shortcuts

Structured unit-price calculations can be run through Siri and Shortcuts on iOS.

Details

Improved layouts for iPhone Duo. History search and selection sheets now respect screen safe areas, and navigation actions adapt better to the sidebar.
