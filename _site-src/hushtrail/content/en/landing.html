Download for Mac Release notes

Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Download the ZIP, unzip it, then drag Hushtrail into Applications. No account required.

Your selected apps

Add apps once and remember your choices. Check only the apps you select for standard system recent documents and supported app history.

Review leftovers

Scan standard locations for possible leftover caches, logs, preferences and app data. Current apps and components are checked before cleanup becomes available.

Choose, then clean

Leftovers start unselected. Select by app or category; app data has an extra confirmation because it may contain personal files. Selected leftovers move to macOS Trash.

Clear reasons

See what can be cleaned and why other items are kept. Unknown ownership, unsupported structures and incomplete checks remain visible.

Native to your Mac

AppKit controls, system language preferences, and a choice of system, light or dark appearance. No startup scan or cleaner dashboard.

Local processing

No document or chat content is read. No analytics SDK or automatic history upload. Contact support only when you choose to.

A focused first release

Initial real-machine testing used macOS 27.0 on Apple silicon. Hushtrail 0.1.3 removes the exact OS-version gate for Finder Recent Folders and experimental Recents in Chosen Folders. The app requires macOS 27.0 or later; current format, file identity and safety checks still determine whether cleanup is available. Not every newer system has been individually tested.

System recent-document cleanup recognizes .sfl4 archives; it does not promise to erase every app’s own history or every Dock menu. IINA playback history has a separate format-checked adapter. Full Disk Access and a macOS administrator prompt may be needed.

Experimental Recents temporarily moves eligible local files while verifying the search index. It excludes cloud/sync folders, packages, links and other disks. Leftovers does not remove drivers, services, whole containers or unidentified vendor folders.

Hushtrail does not make backup copies, provide app-managed restore, empty Trash, or clean browser history and cookies. History removal can be irreversible; documents and media are kept.
