FIRE Support

Start small. Keep the number honest. Let the widget do the reminding.

FIRE 1.4.0 is released for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Requires iOS / iPadOS 18, macOS 15 or watchOS 10. Existing settings are preserved when upgrading.

Share progress

On iPhone, iPad and Mac 1.3, open Share progress from Progress. The initial design follows the app's default style, not a separately configured widget. Choose a free or already unlocked style, size and appearance; enlarge the preview before using the system sharing controls. Amounts start hidden every time you open sharing, and you can hide your note too. The image uses the saved snapshot from opening the page. Exporting or canceling does not change your goal or default style. On Mac, you can also choose Copy Image.

Review this month

On iPhone, iPad and Mac 1.3, This month shows confirmed-record count and the actual date range. Progress changes compare the first and last records in the latest consecutive segment with the same target amount and currency. A target or currency change starts a new segment; with only one comparable record, a change is not yet shown. These are percentage-point changes in goal progress, not investment returns, and no missing month-start balance is invented.

Favorites are free in iPhone, iPad and Mac 1.2. Tap the heart in a style detail, then choose Favorites in the gallery. Favorites stay on this device and do not change your default style or unlock a purchase.

Set up a goal

Enter current assets, a target and a common currency. Approximate amounts are fine. The optional target helper uses annual spending divided by a withdrawal-rate assumption; applying it only fills the draft until you save.

Why isn't progress changing?

Recorded progress changes only when you confirm a new amount. Add a monthly or yearly accumulation plan if you want estimates, then choose estimated progress or estimated time remaining in the widget's configuration. Estimates do not include returns or inflation.

Enter your total balance as usual, or enter just how much it went up or down. When you enter a change, you’ll see the resulting balance and progress toward your goal before you save. If your balance changes while you’re editing, FIRE shows the latest balance and asks you to check it before saving. After you save, the overview shows how much your balance changed and how many percentage points you gained or lost since your last entry for the same goal.

Add or change a widget

Use the system widget gallery and search for FIRE. Choose a size, then edit that widget to select a style or content. Setting the app default does not override a widget with its own style. The system schedules refreshes; changes may not appear immediately.

Touch and hold a FIRE widget on iPhone or iPad, or Control-click it on Mac, then edit the widget. Set a FIRE widget to “Record progress” to go straight to a new entry. Each widget has its own setting and opens your progress overview by default.

Sync and Apple Watch

On first use, choose whether to enable iCloud for your current goal. History sync is a separate choice. Enable sync on devices using the same Apple Account to continue your record; updates are checked when opening the app, saving or manually refreshing and are not guaranteed in real time. In new iPhone installations of FIRE 1.1, a paired Apple Watch with FIRE installed automatically receives saved goal updates when the connection is available. Existing users keep their choice. Turn automatic updates off or resend manually in Settings > Apple Watch. A queued update is not confirmation of delivery; already queued updates may still arrive after switching it off. Watch does not connect directly to iCloud. In FIRE 1.0, automatic Apple Watch updates are off by default.

Privacy on screen

FIRE does not have a manual hide-details switch or app lock. Information may remain visible in the app, screenshots and app-switcher previews. Widgets still respect the system’s privacy-redaction requests.

Support purchases

Core features, iCloud sync and 22 widget styles remain free, with no subscription. iPhone, iPad and Mac 1.2 offer Sunday, Far Afield, Long Summer and Winter Retreat as separate one-time purchases. Any coffee or Supporter Badge purchase includes all current and future premium styles. Restore Purchases checks verified past purchases, including historical coffee support, to recover eligible style access; it does not restore or refund a consumed coffee purchase. The collectible Supporter Badge requires the badge purchase. Prices come from the App Store, and unlocking a style does not apply it automatically.

Contact & feedback

Email and localized Tally forms are optional. The form includes technical context such as product, version, build, system and interface language, plus information needed by the browser and Tally to handle the request. It does not automatically attach amounts, goals, notes, screenshots, logs or stable device identifiers. Your feedback and optional reply email are sent only when you submit.
