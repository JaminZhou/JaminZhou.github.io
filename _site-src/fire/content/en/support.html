FIRE Support

Start small. Keep the number honest. Let the widget do the reminding.

FIRE 1.2.0 has been released for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. App Store updates are still rolling out; some storefronts may show 1.1.0 for now. Mac remains on 1.1.0; the Mac 1.2.0 update is awaiting review. The details below describe this release; existing settings are preserved when upgrading. FIRE 1.1 requires iOS / iPadOS 18, macOS 15 or watchOS 10.

Favorites are free in iPhone and iPad 1.2. Tap the heart in a style detail, then choose Favorites in the gallery. Favorites stay on this device and do not change your default style or unlock a purchase.

Set up a goal

Enter current assets, a target and a common currency. Approximate amounts are fine. The optional target helper uses annual spending divided by a withdrawal-rate assumption; applying it only fills the draft until you save.

Why isn't progress changing?

Recorded progress changes only when you confirm a new amount. Add a monthly or yearly accumulation plan if you want estimates, then choose estimated progress or estimated time remaining in the widget's configuration. Estimates do not include returns or inflation.

Add or change a widget

Use the system widget gallery and search for FIRE. Choose a size, then edit that widget to select a style or content. Setting the app default does not override a widget with its own style. The system schedules refreshes; changes may not appear immediately.

Sync and Apple Watch

On first use, choose whether to enable iCloud for your current goal. History sync is a separate choice. Enable sync on devices using the same Apple Account to continue your record; updates are checked when opening the app, saving or manually refreshing and are not guaranteed in real time. In new iPhone installations of FIRE 1.1, a paired Apple Watch with FIRE installed automatically receives saved goal updates when the connection is available. Existing users keep their choice. Turn automatic updates off or resend manually in Settings > Apple Watch. A queued update is not confirmation of delivery; already queued updates may still arrive after switching it off. Watch does not connect directly to iCloud. In FIRE 1.0, automatic Apple Watch updates are off by default.

Privacy on screen

FIRE does not have a manual hide-details switch or app lock. Information may remain visible in the app, screenshots and app-switcher previews. Widgets still respect the system’s privacy-redaction requests.

Support purchases

Core features, iCloud sync and 22 widget styles remain free, with no subscription. iPhone and iPad 1.2 offer Sunday, Far Afield, Long Summer and Winter Retreat as separate one-time purchases. Mac 1.1 offers Sunday and Long Summer. Any coffee or Supporter Badge purchase includes all current and future premium styles. Restore Purchases checks verified past purchases, including historical coffee support, to recover eligible style access; it does not restore or refund a consumed coffee purchase. The collectible Supporter Badge requires the badge purchase. Prices come from the App Store, and unlocking a style does not apply it automatically.

Contact & feedback

Email and localized Tally forms are optional. The form includes technical context such as product, version, build, system and interface language, plus information needed by the browser and Tally to handle the request. It does not automatically attach amounts, goals, notes, screenshots, logs or stable device identifiers. Your feedback and optional reply email are sent only when you submit.
