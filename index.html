How I build

Three rules that decide what goes in and, more often, what stays out.

Focused scope

Each app is built around one clear job, with complexity kept behind a calm surface.

Platform-aware by design

Controls, navigation, and conventions should feel at home on the platforms they run on.

Reversible and trustworthy

Potentially destructive workflows should explain what will happen and provide a safe path back.
